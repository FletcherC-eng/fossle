        How to play
        • Stack the eight events like layers of rock: the newest at the top, the oldest at the bottom.
        • Drag a card up or down to move it, and the others slide out of the way. You can also tap a card, then tap the spot it should go. Shuffle mixes them all up.
        • Stuck? You get up to 3 hints: a nudge about one card, then clues linking two more cards to famous events, then three more. Tap the bulb to open your hints, then Get a hint for a new one. A bulb appears on the dig where you used one.
        • You get 5 digs. After each one you see how many cards are in the right layer, but not which ones.
        • Tap a past dig at the bottom of the screen to see the order you tried. Tap "Use this order" to go back to it.
        • Keyboard: focus a card, press Enter to pick it up and Enter on another to swap, or use Alt + ↑ / ↓.
        • A new dig opens every day at midnight.
